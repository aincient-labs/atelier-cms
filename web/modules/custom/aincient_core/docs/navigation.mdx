## The room switcher

The switcher in the top bar moves you between rooms:

- **Pages** — your page and content room.
- **Library** — your media.
- **Site** — a menu holding **Identity**, **Navigation & Pages**, **Settings** and **Components**.

The Atelier wordmark returns you home to the General chat.

## Deep links

Every room, page, and item has its own shareable, reload-safe URL. A Fernway page might open at a URL like `/atelier/content/node/5`, so you can bookmark or share exactly where you are and come back to the same spot.

## Moving between the console and your live site

Three affordances follow one rule: a jump to the live site opens a **new tab** — so your unsaved draft and open conversation stay put — while moving around inside the workspace stays in the **same tab**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="View site">
    The globe in the top bar opens your live site in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The editor pill">
    On the live site, an operator sees a small pill. On one of your pages it reads **Edit in Console** and jumps to that page in the **Pages** room; elsewhere it reads **Open Console**. Visitors never see it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="System">
    The account menu has a **System** door to the technical back office at `/admin`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When a link can't open

If you open a deep link to a page you can't access or that no longer exists, the console shows a short recovery screen with a way to start fresh — never a blank editor.
