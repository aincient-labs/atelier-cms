<Note>
  The assistant never publishes on its own. Every edit — from chat or from the editor — updates
  an **unsaved draft** and re-renders the preview. Your page goes live only when you click
  **Publish**.
</Note>

## The structured editor

In the **Pages** room, the structured editor lists your page's sections in order. Each
section is a collapsible card: expand one to edit its fields, collapse it to scan the whole
page. It sits beside the live preview, so you always see what your change does.

Reach for the editor when you know exactly what to change — a headline, a price, the order
of two sections — and want to change it directly instead of describing it in chat.

## Add, remove, and reorder sections

You shape the page from the editor as well as from chat:

- **Add** a section from the component library at any position.
- **Remove** a section you no longer want.
- **Reorder** sections with the move buttons, or by dragging a card up or down.
- **Collapse** or **expand** each card to focus on the section you're working on.

The preview re-renders the moment you make a change, so reordering the Fernway Botanical
Studio hero above its "Meet the growers" story is instant and visible.

## Edit a section's props

Open a card and you edit that section's fields directly. Depending on the section, you'll
find:

- **Tone** — an enum control with `default`, `muted`, `brand`, and `inverted`. Adjacent
  sections auto-alternate so a page never blurs together.
- **Variant** — an enum control for a section's named arrangement, such as a split or
  centered hero.
- **Anchor** — a slug (`pricing`) that makes the section a link target: any link field can
  point at it as `#pricing`. Available on every section, under Appearance.
- **Repeatable rows** — add, remove, and reorder items like feature cards or pricing tiers.
- **Accordion panels** — the expandable panels of an accordion section.
- **Media and block pickers** — choose an image or a reusable block by reference.
- **Multiline text** — longer copy fields such as a subheading or body paragraph.
- **Toggles** — on/off switches for a section's options.

For example, set the Fernway hero's **variant** to split, its **tone** to `brand`, and add
two rows to the features grid ("Repotting clinic", "Plant-sitting") — all without typing a
sentence of chat.

## Click the preview to jump to a section

You don't have to hunt through the card list. Click any section in the live preview and its
card opens and scrolls into view in the editor, ready to edit. It's the fastest way to go
from "that band looks wrong" to changing it.

## Track and revert unsaved changes

Every unsaved edit is marked so you always know what you've touched:

- A per-field **accent dot** marks a field you've changed but not yet published.
- You can **revert a single field** back to its published value without losing your other
  edits.
- Each card shows its own **dirty count**, and the room shows a **total** across the page.

Nothing is saved until you **Publish**. Until then the live site is untouched, and you can
revert field by field or **Discard** the whole draft.
