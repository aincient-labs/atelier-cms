<Note>
  Menus are yours to edit. The assistant won't add, rename, or reorder navigation links — it shapes the page content and brand around them.
</Note>

## Open the menu editor

From the top nav, open the **Site** menu, choose **Navigation & Pages**, then switch to the **Header** or **Footer** tab. Each tab holds an inline editor for that menu.

## Manage links

In either tab you can:

- **Add** a new link.
- **Rename** its label.
- **Re-point** it at a different destination.
- **Reorder** links by dragging them, or with move up / move down.
- **Nest** a link under another by dropping it on that link's submenu button, or with **Nest under previous link**. **Move out of submenu** (or dropping it on a breadcrumb) takes it back up a level.
- **Show or hide** a link with the **Shown** toggle.
- **Remove** a link entirely.

## Point a link at a page or a URL

Each link targets one of two things. Flip the **URL ｜ Page** switch under its label to choose:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Page" icon="file">
    Pick one of your published pages. The link then **tracks** that page — it always points at the page's current published URL, so renaming or re-aliasing the page keeps the menu correct on its own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="URL" icon="link">
    Type any address — a path like `/workshops`, or a full external link like `https://…`.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For Fernway Botanical Studio, you might set a **Page** link to your Care Guides page and a **URL** link to your Instagram — side by side in the same header.

## Nest menus as deep as you like

Menus can nest to any depth. Use a link's **go-in** control — the chevron with a child-count badge — to drill into its children. Drag a link onto another link's go-in control to nest it there. A breadcrumb like `Header menu › Workshops` shows where you are, and you step back up through it.

On the published site, nesting renders automatically:

- A **header** parent opens a drop-down of its children.
- **Footer** children group as a column under their parent.

## Add a page from the page itself

The common case — "put this page in the menu" — doesn't need the menu editor. Open the page in the **Pages** room and use **Add to menu** in its side panel: pick the **Main** or **Footer** menu, and where it goes (top level, or under one of the links already there). The link is added to the menu's **draft**, not published: choose **Review in Navigation** to see it in place, then publish from there. If the page is already in that menu, it tells you instead of adding it twice.

The draft lives in your browser tab until you publish or discard it, so publish before you reload the page.

## Publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit the menu">
    Add, reorder, and re-point links in the Header or Footer tab. Your changes stay in an unsaved draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish**. The header and footer update **site-wide** immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use **Page** links wherever you can. Because they follow the page, you can rename or re-alias a page later without ever coming back to fix a broken menu item.
</Tip>
