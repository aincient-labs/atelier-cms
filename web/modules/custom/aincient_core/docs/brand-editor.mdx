## Where the editor lives

Open the **Site** menu in the top nav and choose **Identity**. This room sets your brand — the colours, type, and shapes every page inherits. The editor sits beside a live preview, so you watch each change land as you make it.

## The four cards

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Color" icon="palette">
    Set your brand, accent, and page colours. Each one carries a live contrast check so you can see at a glance whether text stays legible on it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Typography" icon="type">
    Pick a font pairing from a gallery — every option is previewed in its real typeface. Then set your body text size and heading weight.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Shape & depth" icon="square">
    Dial in corner roundness, spacing density, and the shadow. The shadow is built from independent dials rather than a fixed preset.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Components" icon="component">
    Fine-tune individual elements — buttons, cards, and the like — once the foundations feel right.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Shadows are dials, not presets

The **Shape & depth** card sets the shadow through independent controls: distance, blur, strength, colour, and the direction the light falls from. Because each is its own dial, you can ask for "softer" or "light from the left" and only that single quality changes — the rest of the shadow stays put.

## High-level first, precise controls behind Advanced

Every card leads with the high-level choices most brands need. The precise, per-token controls tuck behind an **Advanced** section on each card, so the surface stays calm until you want to go deeper.

## Preview, revert, publish

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change something">
    Adjust a colour, font, or dial and the preview updates live. A dirty-count badge tracks how many unsaved edits you have.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revert if needed">
    Roll back a single field on its own, or **Discard** the whole draft to return to what's currently live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Nothing is saved until you click **Publish**. Only then does your brand go live across the site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The assistant never publishes on its own. Whether you edit by hand or by chat, you're working on an **unsaved draft** — your brand changes go live only when you press **Publish**.
</Note>

## Edit by hand, by chat, or both

The assistant reaches for these exact same choices when you describe a change in chat. Ask Fernway Botanical Studio's assistant to "warm up the accent green," then open the **Color** card and nudge it further by hand. The two ways of working share one draft, so you can move between them freely.

<Tip>
  Watch the dirty-count badge before you leave the room — it's your reminder that there are unsaved edits waiting for a **Publish** or a **Discard**.
</Tip>
