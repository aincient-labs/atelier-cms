**Settings** holds the few site-wide switches that aren't about how the site looks: the address
system mail comes from, how your brand fonts reach visitors, snapshots of the published site,
and the example content a fresh site starts with. Open it from the **Site** menu in the
console's top bar.

## Site email

The address system mail is sent **from**: password resets, account notices and other
notifications. This is the "from" identity people see on those messages. How the mail is
actually delivered is set up separately; see [Email delivery](/email).

## Font delivery

Choose how your brand fonts reach your public pages:

- **Self-host (private)** — fonts are served from your own site. Nothing is requested from a
  third party, so no consent banner is shown.
- **Load from Google Fonts** — visitors are asked for consent first. Until they accept, the
  system font is shown and nothing is sent to Google.

More in [Privacy & consent](/privacy).

## Snapshots

Freeze the published site so visitors see a fixed version while you keep editing, and switch
back to the live site whenever you like. See [Freeze & Live](/freeze-and-live).

## Example content

A new site can start with example pages and blocks. While any are left, Settings shows an
**Example content** section with **Remove all examples**.

## Publish

<Note>
  Changes to the site email and font delivery are a **draft** until you press **Publish**.
  Snapshots act right away: freezing or switching versions does not wait for Publish.
</Note>

## Not here

- The front page and the 404 and 403 pages are chosen in

- The site name, tagline, logo and favicon are in [Identity](/brand-studio).
- Accounts and roles are in the [System back-office](/system).
