The console is where you run Atelier. It's a chat workspace at `/atelier`: you talk to an
assistant, it does the work, and a live preview shows you the result. You're always in exactly
one **room** — a focused workspace for one kind of task — and you switch between them as
your work changes.

## Rooms

A room pairs a chat with the right assistant and the right preview for one job. You move between
rooms from the top bar, and the Atelier wordmark always takes you home to the **General**
chat.

In the top bar, **Pages** and **Library** sit alongside a **Site** menu that groups the four
set-up rooms — **Identity**, **Navigation & Pages**, **Settings** and **Components** — since
you visit those less often. Each
room keeps its own chat history and its own new conversations, so switching rooms doesn't
tangle your work. When a room offers more than one assistant, a picker at the top of the chat
lets you choose which one a new conversation uses.

## How a conversation works

You describe what you want in plain language. The assistant replies with one short framing
sentence and gets to work — it doesn't narrate every step. As it works:

- The **preview** updates live so you see the real result, not a description of it.
- The assistant works on an **unsaved draft**, never your live site.
- You keep refining in chat, or edit the draft directly in the room's structured editor.

Each conversation is a **thread**. When its work is done — for example when you publish a new
page — the thread wraps up and you start a fresh one for the next change.

## The approval model

The console is built around one rule: **the assistant proposes, you approve.** Nothing reaches
your live site without an explicit action from you.

<Note>
  The assistant has no power to publish on its own. Brand directions and page layouts stay drafts
  until you press **Publish**.
</Note>

Drafts and previews keep the assistant fast while you stay the publisher, and any step that would
change your site pauses for your approval first.

## Moving between the console and your site

The console is your workspace; the live site is what it produces. A jump to the live site
opens a **new tab** (so your unsaved draft and open conversation stay put), while moving
around inside the workspace stays in the **same tab**. The globe in the top bar opens your
live site any time, and on the live site an operator sees a small **Edit in Console** pill to
jump back. The account menu (your avatar, top-right) also has a **System** door to the
technical back office at `/admin` — you rarely need it; the console is home.

## What you can do here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your AI">
    On a fresh install the [onboarding wizard](/onboarding) walks you through connecting a
    provider and choosing your models.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your brand">
    Open the [Identity room](/brand-studio) and describe the identity you want.
    Preview, then publish — it flows through to every page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build pages">
    Under **Pages**, click **New page**, give it a title, and you land in the [Pages
    room](/page-studio) ready to build. Describe the page and the assistant composes it from the
    [component library](/components), live on your brand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage content">
    In the General room, ask the assistant to create, update, or find content. The same
    approval model applies to anything that changes your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>
