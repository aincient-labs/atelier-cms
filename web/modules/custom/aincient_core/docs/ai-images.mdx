## Connect an image provider first

Image generation runs on a connected provider. A Google Gemini key powers Nano Banana image generation, which you connect during setup — see [onboarding](/onboarding). Once a provider is connected, the AI rail appears in the [Library](/media-studio) and in the section you're working on.

## Generate an image from a prompt

Describe what you want and Atelier creates it. For Fernway Botanical Studio you might ask for "a sunlit shelf of trailing pothos in terracotta pots." The new image lands in your [Library](/media-studio) and drops into the section you're working on, ready to preview.

## Edit an existing image in plain language

Point the assistant at an image and describe the change — for example:

- "make it warmer"
- "remove the background"
- "crop to a square"

Atelier produces a **new** image from your request. It never overwrites the original, so you can keep both and compare. If you prefer the edited version, use **Replace original with this** to swap it in.

## Where generated images are saved

A generated or edited image is saved to your **Library** the moment it's ready — as a new item, opened in the editor for you to review. This is deliberate, and it's different from how the assistant edits pages or your brand.

Image generation runs on your connected provider and costs real money each time. So instead of holding the result somewhere temporary that a refresh or a wrong turn could wipe out, Atelier keeps what you paid to create. You'll never lose an image to a stray click.

<Note>
  Saved to your **Library** is not the same as live on your **site**. A Library image is private to your console. It reaches visitors only when you place it on a page and click **Publish** — the same guarantee that applies everywhere in Atelier.
</Note>

## It's still your decision, not the assistant's

Saving the image to your Library doesn't take the choice away from you. You stay in control:

- **Nothing is ever overwritten.** Every generation and every edit is a *new* item. The assistant doesn't touch, replace, or delete anything you already have.
- **Keep it, or don't.** Review the result in the editor. Keep it, ask for another variation, or click **Delete** to discard it — deleting a freshly generated image is safe and removes it completely.
- **Swap it in only if you choose.** For an edit, **Replace original with this** repoints the original — and only when you press it.

<Tip>
  Iterate freely. Because every edit makes a new image instead of replacing the last one, you can try "warmer," then "brighter," then go back — nothing is lost, and nothing you don't want sticks around.
</Tip>
