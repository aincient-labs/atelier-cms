Every name you see in Atelier, what it means, and where to read more. Older names are listed so
you can match what you remember to what the console shows now.

## Assistant

The AI you chat with in a room. Each room has its own assistant; it proposes changes and you publish them.

More: [Assistant](/approval-model).

## Atelier desktop app

The app that installs, updates, backs up and runs Atelier on your computer.

Also called **Atelier Manager**. Formerly called **Atelier app**. More: [Atelier desktop app](/quickstart).

## Checks

Page health audits (SEO, meta tags, internal links) with fixes staged as drafts. Off by default; an operator switches it on.

Formerly called **Checks studio** or **Page audit agent**. More: [Checks](/checks).

## Components

Which section types (hero, gallery, pricing, …) the site offers, with their tones and variants. New components come from developer-built packs.

Formerly called **Components studio**. More: [Components](/components).

## Desktop app's Backups tab

The desktop app's tab for making a backup of the whole site and restoring one.

More: [Desktop app's Backups tab](/backups).

## Desktop app's System tab

The desktop app's tab with Your installation, Check for updates and the update banner.

Formerly called **Settings → Your installation**. More: [Desktop app's System tab](/quickstart).

## Freeze & Live

Snapshots of the published site that visitors are served while you keep editing.

Formerly called **Freeze and Live**. More: [Freeze & Live](/freeze-and-live).

## General

The console's home room, reached by clicking the site's wordmark. Its assistant answers questions and hands work to the other rooms.

Formerly called **General studio**. More: [General](/console).

## Identity

The site's look and voice: colours, typography, shape, name, tagline, logo and favicon.

Formerly called **Identity studio**, **Brand Studio**, **Brand studio** or **Design System studio**. More: [Identity](/brand-studio).

## Library

The shelf of reusable images and blocks. Generate, edit and describe images there.

Formerly called **Library studio**, **Media studio** or **Media library**. More: [Library](/media-studio).

## Navigation & Pages

The main and footer menus, the front page and 404 page, and how the header and footer are arranged.

Shown as **Navigation** where space is short. Formerly called **Navigation & Pages studio**, **Globals** or **Globals studio**. More: [Navigation & Pages](/globals-studio).

## Pages

Build and manage the site's pages beside a live preview. Its assistant composes pages section by section.

Formerly called **Pages studio**, **Page Studio** or **Content studio**. More: [Pages](/page-studio).

## Room

One part of your studio for one job, such as Pages, Library or Identity. Each room has its own chat and assistant.

More: [Room](/console).

## Settings

Site email, privacy and consent, and Freeze & Live snapshots.

Formerly called **Settings studio**. More: [Settings](/site-settings).

## Site

The small menu in the top bar that opens the four set-up rooms: Identity, Navigation & Pages, Settings and Components.

Formerly called **Site Information**. More: [Site](/console).

## Studio

Your studio is the whole Atelier workspace at /atelier: the top bar, its rooms, and the chat.

More: [Studio](/console).

## System back-office

A few technical settings (people, roles) outside the console, opened from the account menu's System door.

More: [System back-office](/system).
