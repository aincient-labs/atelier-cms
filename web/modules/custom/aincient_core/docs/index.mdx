Atelier CMS by AIncient Labs is an AI-first, chat-driven site builder built on
[Drupal](https://www.drupal.org). You build and brand your site by talking to an assistant in a
console, watch every change render live, and publish only what you approve.

It is a full CMS underneath — Drupal core, your content as real nodes, config as code — with
a modern chat console layered on top. You don't fill in admin forms. You describe what you
want, and the assistant assembles it from a curated component library on your brand.

## AI proposes, you approve

Most agentic tools race to act on their own: run with broad permissions, change live
configuration, mutate the running system, and ask forgiveness later. Atelier is the
deliberate opposite.

<Note>
  The assistant **never changes your live site on its own.** It works on an unsaved draft and
  shows you a live preview. Your site changes only when **you** press **Publish**.
</Note>

This is an architectural stance, not a setting you can switch off:

- **No autonomous writes.** The assistant proposes brand directions and page layouts as drafts.
  Persisting them is always a human action.
- **Everything is previewed first.** You see the real result, rendered in your brand, before
  anything is saved.
- **A person is always in the loop.** Actions that change your site pause for your explicit
  approval — nothing ships while you're looking away.

The result is the speed of an assistant with the control of a human editor. AI does the
assembling; you stay the publisher.
