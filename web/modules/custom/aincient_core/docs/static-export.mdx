## What a static export is

A static export is a self-contained copy of your published site as ordinary files. Drop the folder onto any static host — or a plain web server, or even a USB stick — and it just works. Nothing needs to keep running behind it.

## What the export includes

When Atelier builds the snapshot, it:

- **Renders every published page** into finished HTML.
- **Warms the right-sized image versions** so photos load fast on every device.
- **Checks internal links** along the way, so you know the snapshot hangs together.

You can also **bundle your configuration and users** alongside the site, so the export carries not just the pages but the setup behind them.

## You own your data

Your content in Atelier is real, structured content — not locked inside a page builder — and your configuration travels with it. That means you can take the whole thing with you at any time:

- Move Fernway Botanical Studio to a different host without a migration project.
- Keep an offline archive of the site as it looked on a given day.
- Hand a client a complete, hostable copy of their site.

## Put it online

Once you have the export, host it anywhere static files are served — Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, GitHub Pages, your own server, or object storage. The [Host your exported site](/deploy) guide walks through each one, step by step.

<Note>
  A static export is a snapshot of your **published** site. Keep editing in the console as usual; export again whenever you want a fresh copy.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Want the appliance itself to serve a snapshot to visitors while you keep editing? That is [Freeze & Live](/freeze-and-live): the same export, kept as versions inside Atelier, switchable in one click.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Owning your data is a feature, not a formality. Because your content and configuration both travel, you're never locked in — Atelier is a place to work, not a place your site is trapped.
</Tip>
