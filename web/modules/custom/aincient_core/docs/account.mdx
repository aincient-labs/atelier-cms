## Your identity card

Open the flyout from your avatar in the top-right. It shows a read-only identity card with your name, email, roles, and status — a quick check of who you're signed in as.

## Manage account

Click your avatar in the top-right of the console and choose **Manage account**. It opens an
in-console pane where you can:

- Change your email.
- Change your password.
- Set your timezone.
- Upload or remove your avatar.

There's no need to leave the console for a Drupal form.

<Note>
Changing your email or password re-verifies your current password on the server for safety. One
exception: right after you sign in with a one-time link (for example **Edit my site** in the Atelier
manager, or `atelier app open`), you can set a password without entering a current one. Use this to get back to password
sign-in when email password reset isn't set up on your site.
</Note>

## One-time sign-in links

Opening a one-time sign-in link while you're already signed in:

- **As the same user** — takes you straight to the console.
- **As someone else** — asks **Switch account?**. Choose **Sign out and continue** to sign in as the
  link's user, or stay signed in as yourself; the link stays usable until it expires.

## Other doors in the menu

The same menu holds two more entries:

- **Set up AI providers** — reopens the onboarding wizard.
- **System** — for site administrators, a door to the technical back office at `/admin`.
