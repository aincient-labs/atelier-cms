**Navigation & Pages** is where you shape what wraps every page: how the **header** and
**footer** are arranged, the links in their menus, and which pages answer at the site root and
when something goes wrong. Edit it directly in the rail, or describe what you want and let the
assistant preview it. Nothing goes live until you click **Publish**.

Formerly called **Globals**.

<Note>
  The assistant never publishes on its own. It edits an **unsaved draft** held in your browser.
  Your changes go live only when you press **Publish**.
</Note>

## Open it

In the console's top bar, open the **Site** menu and choose **Navigation & Pages** (on a phone
the crumb reads **Navigation**). You get a live preview of your header and footer around a
placeholder page, a chat beside it, and an editor rail with three tabs.

## The three tabs

  <Card title="Header" icon="panel-top">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Footer" icon="panel-bottom">

The site name, tagline, logo and favicon that the header and footer show are set in
[Identity](/brand-studio). The site email address and font privacy are in **Settings**.

## Front page, 404 and 403

On the **Pages** tab, point each slot at a page you've built:

- **Front page** — the page shown at your site's root address.
- **Not-found page (404)** — shown when a visitor opens an address that doesn't exist.
- **Access-denied page (403)** — shown when a visitor isn't allowed to see a page.

Pick any published page from the same picker used everywhere else in the console. Every slot is
optional: an empty slot uses the default, clearing it puts the default back, and deleting a page
a slot points at safely resets that slot. On **Publish** the choice takes effect immediately.

<Tip>
  Build a friendly 404 that links back to your best pages. A lost visitor is one good link away
  from staying.
</Tip>

## Editing by chat

Describe what you want and the assistant previews it instantly — for example:

- *"Centre the logo and stop the header sticking."*
- *"Make the logo bigger."*
- *"Make the footer stacked and hide its tagline."*

The assistant applies its change to the **same draft** the rail edits, so the live preview
re-renders and the edited fields light up in the rail with an unsaved-change count. Keep
refining in chat or tweak the fields directly, then **Publish** when you're happy, or
**Discard** to go back to what is live.

## Menus are yours to edit

The assistant arranges the header and footer, but it does **not** edit your menus. You manage
header and footer links yourself in the menu editor on the **Header** and **Footer** tabs: add,
rename, re-point, reorder or remove a link. If you ask the assistant to change a link, it
points you to the menu editor. More in [Menus](/menus).

### Link to a page, or to any URL

Each link targets one of two things — flip the **URL ｜ Page** switch under its label:

- **Page** — pick one of your published pages. The link then **tracks that page**: it always
  points at the page's current published URL, so renaming or re-aliasing the page keeps the
  menu correct with no extra work.
- **URL** — type any address yourself: a path on your own site (`/about`, `/pricing`) or a full
  external link (`https://…`).

### Nested menus (sub-menus)

Menus can nest as deep as you like. To give a link sub-items, use its **go-in** control
(the chevron with a child-count badge). The editor drills into that link and shows just its
children, with a breadcrumb (e.g. `Header menu › Products`) to step back up.

On the published site, nesting renders automatically: in the **header** a parent link opens a
drop-down (on hover and on keyboard focus); in the **footer** children group as a column under
their parent.
